A startup raised $120M. Three years later, it has 4 employees left.

The full breakdown behind the Zupee data-decode carousel: every source, every date, and three more case studies in the same pattern.

The Primary Case: Zupee

Zupee, the Delhi-NCR gaming startup, raised more than $120 million across its lifetime. According to an Inc42 exclusive published November 13, 2026 (corroborated by Newzchain, Chipdispatch, and SignalDesk), the company has gone through three separate layoff rounds and now operates with 3-4 people, mostly the founders.

Round 1 (early 2025)-170 staff
Round 2 (December 2025)-200 staff
Round 3 (October 2026)Entire remaining team
"AI pivot" announced16 Oct 2026

Peak headcount was close to 500. It did not collapse in one dramatic layoff; it bled out over roughly 18 months, each round smaller than the excuse that preceded it.

The Ecosystem Context

This is not an isolated story. Per Entrackr's Q3 2026 funding report (via Forbes India, Nov 12, 2026) and the Layoffs.fyi tracker, at least 6 funded Indian startups shut down entirely in that quarter alone.

A funding round is a bet on the future. A layoff round is an admission about the past. Watch what a company does with its headcount, not what it says in its press release.

Money raised was never proof of a working business. It's runway, bought on a story. What happens when the story runs out is the only number that actually matters.
